# Social icons

Instagram, Pinterest, YouTube and LinkedIn use [Bootstrap Icons](https://github.com/twbs/icons), licensed under the MIT license. The complete license is in `LICENSE-bootstrap-icons.txt`.

The icons are embedded as inline SVGs in the HTML pages so they remain sharp and inherit the website's color and hover styles. This folder preserves their attribution and license.

Social links retain their existing placeholder destinations until confirmed profile URLs are supplied. The current contact email is preserved during the rebrand.
